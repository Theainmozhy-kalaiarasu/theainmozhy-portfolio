I'm a full-stack designer obsessed with crafting digital experiences that genuinely empower people. Inspired by curiosity and creativity, I turn fresh ideas into thoughtful products that feel intuitive, human, and a little bit magical.

AT THE MOMENT

Every day is a mix of experiments, pixels, and "wait, what if we tried this?" moments that turn vague ideas into products people actually enjoy using. I live for the 0→1 rush of building something from nothing—diving into research, obsessing over user journeys, ideating wildly, prototyping fast, testing harder, and collaborating with anyone who will nerd out with me. As a UX designer, I'm annoyingly serious about clarity, accessibility, and flow, making sure every interaction feels intentional, effortless, and just a bit delightful.
